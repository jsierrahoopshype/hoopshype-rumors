NBA Rumors Wrap: Giannis trade talks heat up on draft eve, Lakers pursue big men and Lu Dort, Wolves gauge Randle and DiVincenzo value The **Giannis Antetokounmpo** trade situation is reaching a fever pitch with the **NBA Draft** hours away. The **Bucks** have drawn criticism from around the league for asking prices that multiple sources describe to Marc Stein as "unrealistic," with Milwaukee GM Jon Horst reportedly seeking returns so steep that any acquiring team would be left too depleted to contend. The **Heat** remain the frontrunner, with Miami reporter Anthony Chiang predicting a deal gets done on draft night, and Heat-linked players **Nikola Jovic**, **Kasparas Jakucionis**, and **Pelle Larsson** have each come up in different iterations of the talks, per HoopsHype. The **Bucks** are also described as aware of how badly Giannis wants to play in Miami, with Barry Jackson reporting Milwaukee appears motivated to leave the Heat in a roster position that makes building around Antetokounmpo difficult regardless of how the trade shakes out. One complicating factor: if Giannis declines to publicly commit to an extension anywhere but Miami, the Bucks face a choice between accepting Miami's offer or gambling that he signs a four-year, $275 million extension on October 1 rather than leaving money on the table. Milwaukee's posture on **Jaylen Brown** is also in flux. If the **Celtics** do acquire Antetokounmpo and Brown ends up in Milwaukee as part of the return, the Bucks would seriously consider keeping him rather than flipping him for picks, contrary to what many around the league assumed. Here are more notes from around the league: • The **Trail Blazers** have been involved in Antetokounmpo talks on two fronts, both as a potential acquiring team and as a facilitator, given Portland holds two future Bucks first-round pick swaps in 2028 and 2030. The Blazers also have serious interest in Brown if Boston were to make him available separately, though Brown is not currently on the market outside of a Giannis deal. • The **Timberwolves** have gauged the trade value of Julius Randle, Donte DiVincenzo, and their No. 28 pick. Minnesota's pursuit of Antetokounmpo appears to have cooled, and the Wolves have also registered strong interest in **Derrick White** from Boston. Minnesota has made **Jaden McDaniels** essentially untradeable unless a blockbuster Giannis or **Nikola Jokic** deal materialized, and rebuffed the Clippers when Los Angeles inquired about McDaniels earlier this season. • The **Lakers** have been linked to center options **Mitchell Robinson** and **Robert Williams III** in free agency, though both players are said to be open to staying with the **Knicks** and Blazers respectively. The Lakers have also called the Thunder about Lu Dort's availability. Multiple front office executives told HoopsHype they expect Oklahoma City to exercise Dort's $17.22 million team option and then consider flipping him on the trade market, with the Lakers and Nets among the teams connected to him. • The **Mavericks** are actively exploring trades while also preparing for picks Nos. 9 and 30 and closing in on a conclusion to their coaching search. Dallas has mutual interest with guard prospect **Brayden Burries** at No. 9, but if the **Clippers** take Burries at No. 5, the Mavericks may be even more inclined to trade down. One name to watch if Dallas slides back: Washington big man **Hannes Steinbach**, whose father played alongside Dirk Nowitzki in Wurzburg decades ago. • The **Clippers** are also said to be interested in Brown and have assets to offer, including the fifth pick and a 2029 unprotected first from the Pacers via the Ivica Zubac trade. The **Nets** have attempted to move up into the top four of the draft by packaging their No. 6 pick with future firsts. • **Collin Gillespie** intends to sign a four-year, $48 million deal to return to the **Suns**, per Shams Charania. Gillespie spent three straight seasons on two-way contracts after going undrafted out of Villanova in 2022 before earning a full roster spot and breaking out last season. The Suns have also done background work on **Bulls** guard **Josh Giddey**, per HoopsHype. • **Isaiah Hartenstein** has a $28.5 million team option with the **Thunder** for next season, but the expectation is that Oklahoma City and Hartenstein will work out a longer-term deal at a lower average salary, allowing the team to reduce its payroll while giving Hartenstein security. Meanwhile, role players **Isaiah Joe** and **Aaron Wiggins** have drawn interest from teams around the league after seeing limited playoff action against San Antonio. • **RJ Barrett** has drawn trade interest in multi-team conversations, with the **Raptors** swingman on an expiring $29.62 million deal. Barrett averaged 20.5 points and 5.9 rebounds last season while shooting 49.5 percent from the field. • **Detroit**'s **Isaiah Stewart** is not just available but bracing for a trade this summer as the Pistons look to add shooting and playmaking. The Bucks are among the teams that have expressed interest in Stewart. Detroit is expected to retain veteran forward Tobias Harris after his solid postseason run. • Minnesota wants to re-sign **Ayo Dosunmu** after acquiring him at the deadline, with his projected market landing north of $18 million annually and potentially reaching the low $20 million range. The **Jazz** have also expressed interest in re-signing Jusuf Nurkic. • The **Hawks** see value in prospect **Dailyn Swain** at No. 23, but have been signaling they prefer to trade the pick for future draft capital and will accept outside offers right up until they're on the clock Tuesday night. • **Luka Doncic** confirmed he will not play for **Slovenia** this summer, skipping both the July window and the World Cup qualifiers in Qatar in August. "If you don't win the title, it's a bad season," Doncic said at an event in Ljubljana. "We've been unlucky with injuries, myself included, after we played some great basketball in March." • **Josh Hart** is publicly waiting on an apology from Becky Hammon after her previous comments about **Jalen Brunson**. Appearing on the Roommates Show, Hart said he was "still waiting for somebody" to admit they were wrong about the player who led New York to a championship, stopping just short of naming Hammon directly before the crowd did it for him.
