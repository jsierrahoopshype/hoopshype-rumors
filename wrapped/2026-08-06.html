NBA Rumors Wrap: Dillon Brooks locks up extension with Suns, Sabonis trade saga detailed, LeBron to run point for Sixers **Dillon Brooks** is staying in Phoenix. The **Suns** forward agreed to a three-year, $73 million contract extension through 2029-30, per Shams Charania. Brooks will earn roughly 13.98 percent of the cap annually in the first year of the deal, slightly more than **Josh Hart** received on his extension and two ticks above what **PJ Washington** got on his. The extension rewards Brooks for becoming a cornerstone of Phoenix's two-way, hard-nosed identity under coach **Jordan Ott**, and it comes after the Suns made the playoffs last season as a surprise entry. Phoenix has built real continuity this offseason, also signing **Collin Gillespie**, **Mark Williams** and **Jordan Goodwin** to multiyear deals while acquiring Miles Bridges and Luke Kennard. Here are more notes from around the league: • The **Kings'** attempts to move **Domantas Sabonis** this summer hit two dead ends. Sacramento nearly traded him to the Raptors at the February deadline in a deal centered on **RJ Barrett**, but **Toronto** couldn't shed the three years and $84 million remaining on **Jakob Poeltl's** contract, and the talks collapsed. This summer, the Kings visited with restricted free agent **Jalen Duren** in early July and explored a sign-and-trade swap of big men, but the **Pistons** wanted no part of losing Duren for Sabonis. • With two years and $94 million left on his deal, Sabonis is returning to Sacramento. The Kings are not exploring an extension despite him becoming extension-eligible in July, and he switched representation to CAA Sports last week. He has told the organization he's willing to recommit, and first-round pick Darius Acuff Jr. says Sabonis texted him after the draft to welcome him and express excitement about their offensive potential together. • The Kings have also maintained interest in free agent wing **Jonathan Kuminga**, but a sign-and-trade with Atlanta is off the table because it would hard cap Sacramento at the first apron. Without that route, they can offer only a minimum, so talks have stalled. • **Nick Nurse** has been fielding congratulatory texts from just about everyone he knows since **LeBron James** signed with the **Sixers**. "Pretty sure every single person I've ever known has texted me or emailed me to say congrats. So that's pretty cool," Nurse said. As for how he plans to use LeBron, Nurse confirmed James will run the point: "He is an offense creator. He is certainly a guy that will bring the ball up the floor and try to create for himself and others." • **Kentavious Caldwell-Pope** has officially signed with Philadelphia and will wear No. 1. • **Stephen Curry** addressed the Warriors' situation during **Moses Moody's** charity event in North Little Rock, Ark. "When Jimmy got hurt and Moses got hurt, the whole calculus of everything changes," Curry said, referencing **Jimmy Butler's** injury last season. He added: "I think honestly, you figure it out in real time." He made clear he doesn't want the Warriors to "suck right now, either," and the conversation between him and the front office about the franchise's direction has been ongoing for years. The emerging consensus around the team is that Curry, **Draymond Green** and coach **Steve Kerr** all understand a championship is likely out of reach, but their priority is going out together with dignity. • **Luka Doncic** is hosting the entire **Lakers** roster in Ljubljana for a four-day minicamp from Aug. 20 to Aug. 24. A source told ESPN the goal is to extend Doncic's time in Slovenia before the season while creating a team-bonding opportunity. • **Victor Wembanyama** spent part of his offseason training at **De'Aaron Fox's** home gym in Texas, with **Tobias Harris** and **Harrison Barnes** also participating. He then linked up with six other Spurs teammates in France for workouts at his former club Nanterre 92, of which he now owns a stake. The contingent, which includes **Stephon Castle**, **Dylan Harper**, **Carter Bryant** and **Jordan McLaughlin**, comprises the majority of San Antonio's projected rotation and is expected to spend around a week training together. Wembanyama also resumed work with **Hakeem Olajuwon**, who helped him develop his post game last summer. • The **Spurs** and San Antonio's new arena are at odds with the mayor's office. Days after Mayor Gina Ortiz Jones called for a public vote on the city's contribution to a $1.3 billion arena project, team CEO **R.C. Buford** published an open letter pushing back. "The vote has taken place. The commitments have been made. The work is underway," Buford wrote, calling it "a responsible public-private partnership built around clear commitments." • Federal prosecutors are seeking to jail **Terry Rozier's** alleged co-conspirator **De'Niro Laster**, accusing him of tampering with witnesses and obstructing justice, including through social media posts. Rozier's attorney David Markus fired back, calling it "a desperate attempt by the government to taint Terry because it sees its case is falling apart." Markus also clarified that the "GMB" group name prosecutors flagged stands for "Get More Blessings," the motto of Rozier's nonprofit foundation. • **Luguentz Dort** posted a heartfelt goodbye to **Oklahoma City** on Instagram after signing with the **Hawks** this offseason. "There are no words that could ever fully express what the 405 means to me," Dort wrote. "I'll forever be grateful." • **Lonnie Walker IV** is returning to the NBA after a stint with Maccabi Tel Aviv in the EuroLeague. He agreed to a one-year, $3.3 million deal with the Nuggets. Meanwhile, the **Clippers** signed guard **Jalen Pickett** to a two-way contract. Pickett, 26, shot 39 percent from three in three seasons with Denver. To make room, the Clippers waived two-way guard Sean Pedulla, who averaged 23.6 points and 6.8 assists per game during the G League regular season and won G League Rookie of the Year. • The **Rockets** waived guard JD Davison, a move that brings them under the luxury tax threshold. • The **Celtics** signed second-round pick **Dillon Mitchell** to a two-way contract. • The NBA is set to release its full 2026-27 schedule next week, per Marc Stein. The league had been planning preseason games in Doha this fall, with the **Nets** and **Wizards** slated to travel there, but those plans have been scrapped given the situation in the Middle East. The **Cavs** and **Heat** had also been exploring a trip to Abu Dhabi as part of the same trip. • Cleveland's new WNBA franchise will be called the **Cleveland Sirens**. The team is set to begin play in 2028 and deliberately chose to distance itself from the Cavaliers' branding. "We don't want to be the Lady Cavaliers," said Sirens exec Allison Howard.
