NBA Rumors Wrap: Spurs reach NBA Finals, Giannis trade market heats up, Knicks' Robinson injury update **Victor Wembanyama** and the **San Antonio Spurs** are headed to the NBA Finals after stunning the **Oklahoma City Thunder** in Game 7 of the Western Conference Finals, capping one of the more remarkable single-season turnarounds in recent memory. The Spurs won just 56 games combined in Wembanyama's first two seasons, then exploded for 74 wins and a Finals berth in year three. Wembanyama was named Western Conference Finals MVP, receiving all nine votes from the media panel. The win was also historic on a broader level: per HoopsHype, no team with a point differential above 11 had ever lost a series to a team with a point differential under 11 until Saturday night, when San Antonio became the first at the 24th attempt. The Spurs will now face the **New York Knicks**, meaning the NBA will have a different champion for the eighth consecutive season, the longest such streak in league history. Here are more notes from around the league: • Wembanyama was candid after the win about what a championship would mean to him. "Winning the Larry O'Brien is a childhood dream," he said. "You never know what's going to happen again. The day we win it, it's going to be an amazing day of realization of a dream. It's almost like the meaning of my life." He also spoke about wanting to reach out to **Gregg Popovich**, who has been away from the team dealing with health issues. "I need to call him. I need to see him," Wembanyama said. "There's no way I can understand right now how he feels." • **Julian Champagnie** was a driving force in Game 7, hitting six three-pointers, the most ever in a Conference Finals Game 7 outside of Stephen Curry's seven. Wembanyama had only praise for his teammate afterward. "He got cut in the NBA a few years back," Wembanyama said. "He's had tough moments. Now he's taking this team to the NBA Finals." • **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** was gracious in defeat, saying the Spurs "were just the better team tonight, from start to finish." He credited San Antonio's makeup, adding: "They're young, talented, well-coached, play the right way, seems like they like each other. You don't beat us without the make-up." When asked how much input he'd have in Oklahoma City's offseason decisions, Gilgeous-Alexander kept it brief: "I will have zero input. I will let Sam Presti, the greatest GM ever, do his job." • **Alex Caruso** pushed back on the idea that the Spurs represent some unsolvable problem for the Thunder. "There's nothing that needs to be solved," he said. "We could've won the game tonight. You would've been asking them the same thing. We've gotta get better and that's on all of us." • **Luke Kornet**'s chase-down block in the series drew comparisons to **LeBron James** on the Spurs bench. "Someone from the bench yelled, 'Who is it? LeBron James?'" Kornet said with a smile. "We'll see which one has more staying power in the record books of history." • With Wembanyama's Spurs now a confirmed dynasty contender, the **Giannis Antetokounmpo** trade market is drawing more attention. Ramona Shelburne said the postseason has completely reframed the trade discussions around the **Milwaukee Bucks** star, arguing that "every single person watching these playoffs says, maybe we better get Giannis" as a counter to what Wembanyama could become. Shelburne separately named the **Orlando Magic** as a surprise team to watch, saying they're "close, already making a move, and going to get a new coach who has some good young pieces." The **Miami Heat** also remain in the conversation: if they don't land Giannis, one analyst said the Heat could pivot to **Kawhi Leonard** or **Ja Morant**. • **Cleveland Cavaliers** president Koby Altman was asked directly at his end-of-season press conference whether **Evan Mobley** would be on the roster next season, a question clearly tied to Giannis speculation. "I mean, he's part of our future," Altman said. "We're not going to speculate on any players outside these walls." He offered no guarantees. • **Donovan Mitchell** spoke at length about his future with the Cavaliers after a painful sweep exit. "I've made Cleveland home outside of just basketball. I don't want to go. I don't really know how else to blatantly say it to people for them to believe me." Mitchell also defended **James Harden**, who factored into the team's run: "We don't get to the conference finals without James Harden." ESPN's Bobby Marks expects Harden to opt out of his $42 million player option this summer, with Cleveland likely negotiating a new two-year deal that could also help the team's cap situation. • **Mitchell Robinson** will try to play in the NBA Finals despite a fracture of the fifth metacarpal in his hand, the bone connecting the wrist to the pinky finger. Average recovery following surgery is around 52 days, but Robinson is expected to push to play through it. The Knicks big man addressed his critics on Instagram, writing: "The ones that want to see me down and hurt, all I gotta say for you is f–k you." • The **New York Knicks** hired **Mike Brown** as head coach, though the process was far from smooth. Marc Stein said Brown was at best the sixth choice for the job, after the Knicks were denied permission to speak with Jason Kidd, Ime Udoka, Quinn Snyder, Chris Finch, and Billy Donovan. Kidd and Donovan both received contract extensions before New York could pursue them, leaving the organization without a clear succession plan after firing **Tom Thibodeau**. • **Karl-Anthony Towns** spoke about his lifelong connection to the Knicks ahead of the Finals, giving a heartfelt shoutout to **Jeremy Lin**. "He really made me a Knicks fan and had me at Modell's," Towns said. "He made me so passionate about the Knicks. It's crazy now that I'm in this position." • **Luka Doncic** has joined an investment group led by former NBA official Donnie Nelson to build a basketball club in Rome. "For me, this is not just a simple financial investment," Doncic said. "I want to build a club that can compete at the highest level and that will mean something to Roma." He also expressed hope that the EuroLeague and NBA can eventually find a way to cooperate. • **Jusuf Nurkic** said he has never received an offer from Partizan Belgrade despite his affinity for the club, and jokingly added "I'm now looking for new jobs" when discussing his upcoming free agency. • The adidas Eurocamp, the top pre-draft showcase for international prospects aged 16-22, runs June 5-7 in Treviso, Italy. **Franz Wagner**, **Bogdan Bogdanovic**, and **VJ Edgecombe** will attend as special guests to share their paths to the NBA with the next generation of international talent.
