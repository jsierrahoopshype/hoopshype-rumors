NBA Rumors Wrap: Karl-Anthony Towns extension stalls, Braiden Bell seeks reinstatement, Luka Doncic impresses in preseason return **Karl-Anthony Towns** made clear Monday morning that his contract extension talks with the **New York Knicks** are not going well, and that may be an understatement. "It don't look good," Towns said before New York's shootaround in Philadelphia, and later added: "Simply put, I want to do something to keep the team together and can work with the Knicks and also values me. And none of those things are close to happening." Towns is eligible for a four-year, $276 million max extension but the Knicks, squeezed against the second apron, have offered roughly four years and $200 million, a figure Towns views as an extreme discount. Per Shams Charania, Towns told the team he is willing to take a considerable discount, as teammates **Mikal Bridges**, **OG Anunoby** and **Jalen Brunson** have done, but the gap between the two sides remains substantial. With the regular season approaching, an extension before opening night now appears unlikely. Here are more notes from around the league: • **Braiden Bell**, the play-by-play announcer hired and fired by the **Portland Trail Blazers** on the same day last week after derogatory old tweets surfaced, has retained legal counsel and is formally asking for reinstatement. "Braiden is not asking anyone to excuse what he wrote," said his attorney Arnie Herz. "He has owned it, without excuses. What he is asking is whether a boy's words from 14 years ago should outweigh everything the man has done since." The Blazers responded with a brief statement: "We stand by our statement issued last week and have no further comment at this time. We wish Mr. Bell the best." • **Luka Doncic** scored 21 points in 16 first-half minutes in the **Lakers**' preseason win over the **Kings**, looking noticeably lighter. Coach **JJ Redick** said: "The guy worked his butt off all summer and actually ran this summer. He actually did like sprints and stuff. Came into camp in fantastic shape." Doncic took a hard knee to his quad midway through the second quarter but stayed in the game, and with the Lakers up 26 at halftime, he sat the second half. • **Dalton Knecht** is in danger of missing the Lakers' regular-season roster entirely, with the team holding 16 guaranteed contracts and room for only 15. **Bronny James** and **Jaden Hardy** have reportedly outplayed him in training camp, making Knecht the odd man out heading into the final roster cuts. • **Max Strus** suffered a partial tear of the plantar fascia in his right foot during Sunday's preseason game and will be reevaluated in four weeks, per Shams Charania. It will be the third straight season Strus has missed time to begin the year, with foot surgery costing him time last year and an ankle sprain doing the same in 2024. **Kris Dunn** or Keaton Wagler could open the season as the **Clippers**' starting guard in Week 1. • **Darius Garland** said the trade away from **Cleveland** still motivates him. "So everybody thought that I was just out of the league basically," Garland said. "But now I'm tryna get that spark back, tryna get back to the all star level that I'm used to being at." He also donated $100,000 to storm relief in Gary, Indiana, his home state, this summer. • **LeBron James** is splitting time between New York and Philadelphia as a **76er**, and his use of a Fanatics-branded helicopter raised questions this week. A spokesperson for James told Front Office Sports the aviation access is part of his broader deal with Fanatics, which predates his playing contract with Philadelphia and extends beyond his playing career. • ESPN is in advanced talks to hire former **Mavericks** coach **Jason Kidd** for NBA studio coverage, per Marc Stein. Kidd was fired by Dallas in May, two weeks after **Masai Ujiri** was hired as president of basketball operations. • **Shai Gilgeous-Alexander** paid tribute to **Russell Westbrook** following his retirement. "He is as dynamic as they come in terms of an NBA player, personality and teammate. Russ is the whole package. He's the foundation for what we stand on now," SGA said. He added it was "a little shocking that it's this soon" given Westbrook averaged 15.2 points, 6.7 assists and 5.4 rebounds in his final season with Sacramento. • **Steve Kerr** on **Stephen Curry**: "I cannot even imagine a coach being luckier ever in the history of the game than I am to coach Stephen Curry, not just the player but the human being." Kerr also revealed that when he asked Curry about potentially leaving the **Warriors**, Curry's first response was: "I'm not moving my family. They're happy here." Kerr said he could feel that Curry genuinely does not want to play anywhere else. • **LaMelo Ball** made his **Minnesota Timberwolves** preseason debut and impressed **Anthony Edwards**, who said: "He plays like his hair is on fire. He gets everybody involved. He talked more than I expected throughout the game." LaMelo said the chemistry will only grow: "The more we play, the easier and easier it's going to get." • **Jordan Hawkins** is signing a two-way deal with the **Chicago Bulls**, per HoopsHype. The former No. 14 pick reunites with Bulls executive Bryson Graham, who was part of the **New Orleans Pelicans** front office that drafted Hawkins in 2023. • The NBA will test wrist wearables for officials in select preseason games this week, allowing referees to communicate with the Replay Center for potential reviews, scoring changes and clock malfunctions. The technology was piloted at Summer League 2026 and will not extend into the regular season. The league is also testing Wilson "connected basketballs" to collect data for future officiating models, though the data will not impact any in-game calls during the preseason. • The NBA's proposed partnership with the **Euroleague** carries a deadline of October 6, with the two key conditions being a response from the league's owners' meeting in Cernobbio and a commitment from the Euroleague to remain at its current 13-club structure, effectively putting its expansion to 24 teams on hold. • **Scottie Barnes** hurt his knee in the first 30 seconds of the **Raptors**' preseason game but played through it for the rest of the half. He said it is sore and he is getting treatment, though he expects to be fine ahead of Toronto's next game Saturday. • **Jrue Holiday** on the **Trail Blazers**' frontcourt: "Everybody knows Dame, everybody knows Ja, everybody knows Deni. We're taking those three, it's a lot of buckets and I think they're going to be hard to stop."
