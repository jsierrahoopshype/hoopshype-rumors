NBA Rumors Wrap: Celtics trade Jaylen Brown to Sixers for Paul George, LeBron's next move takes shape, Nuggets add Bagley

The NBA's offseason went nuclear Wednesday when Shams Charania reported that the Boston Celtics had agreed to trade Jaylen Brown to the Philadelphia 76ers for Paul George, two first-round picks and two second-round picks. Brown, the 2024 Finals MVP, never asked out, per multiple reports — this was entirely a Boston front office decision, one apparently driven by the belief that the Celtics were better with him off the court than on it over his decade with the team. The Sixers sent back a 2028 first that could convert to a swap favorable to Boston, a 2031 unprotected Philadelphia first, and two second-rounders. The Celtics set an astronomical asking price early, per Charania, wanting four or five firsts plus a player like VJ Edgecombe if Philadelphia was offering George, but the Sixers stayed in the conversation long enough to get a deal done. Chris Haynes said Boston views this as their "super move" and expects only peripheral additions from here.

Windhorst reported the trade was made "under duress" — his words were that "this was not a trade that you would make in a clinical setting" and that "something wasn't equal." The Celtics, per Windhorst, felt Derrick White had a better season than Brown last year, even as Brown finished sixth in MVP voting. Per HoopsHype, Brown is the second-fastest Finals MVP to be traded after winning the award, behind Dennis Johnson, who went from the 1979 championship to Phoenix the following year.

Here are more notes from around the league:
  • • LeBron James is in Ohio on family time with no real momentum yet on his free agency. Windhorst said teams have expressed interest but there has not been traction on what a process might look like, whether meetings will happen, or even what LeBron's timeline is. Shams said LeBron's priority is finding a team that gives him a genuine shot at a championship.
  • • The Nuggets, Warriors, Cavaliers and Heat are the teams actively pursuing James, per Marc Stein. The Spurs are not expected to be in the mix after signing Tobias Harris, and the 76ers have also expressed interest. Windhorst has long believed Denver is the most natural outlier fit given a potential pairing with Nikola Jokic, and said he now thinks the Nuggets should get in front of LeBron or his agent Rich Paul and make their pitch.
  • • Per HoopsHype, LeBron is the first Laker in franchise history to leave in free agency the offseason after averaging at least 20 points per game. He also becomes just the second Laker to leave as a reigning All-Star, joining Dwight Howard. Paul told the New York Post that LeBron told him, "I'm 100% sure that's not a place I would want to return," ending any need for a formal meeting with Los Angeles.
  • • The Lakers are moving forward. According to The Athletic, the team is still seeking a young wing for perimeter defense, with former Warrior and Hawk Jonathan Kuminga receiving significant consideration. Atlanta declined his $24.3 million team option. Austin Reaves posted a goodbye to James on Instagram Stories: "Thank you for everything. See you on the golf course soon brother!"
  • • The Nuggets signed free agent center Marvin Bagley III to a one-year deal, per Shams Charania. Bagley's agents at Klutch Sports sorted through several offers before landing on Denver, which Charania described as a strong opportunity with a West contender for the former No. 2 overall pick.
  • • Memphis is trading Santi Aldama to Dallas for AJ Johnson, a protected 2030 Warriors first-round pick and two future second-rounders. The deal lets the Grizzlies preserve their full $29 million trade exception from the Jaren Jackson Jr. trade, which would have been reduced by their earlier offseason moves. Dallas also acquires the draft rights to Tarik Biberovic in the deal. With Aldama heading to the Mavs, it is increasingly unlikely Dwight Powell returns for a 13th season in Dallas.
  • • The Mavericks are also expected to add former Pelicans coach Willie Green as a top assistant on Dusty May's first NBA staff. The Warriors had hoped to bring Green back for a second stint under Steve Kerr, but May's move to the NBA gave Green two attractive options.
  • • The Bucks engaged Memphis on potential Ja Morant trades as early as January, but chose not to go through with a deal as of Monday, per The Athletic. The Trail Blazers ultimately landed Morant.
  • • Tim Hardaway Jr. is heading to Miami on a one-year, $6.5 million deal, returning to the city where his father became a Heat legend. He won't wear No. 10, though — that number is retired for Tim Hardaway Sr. and hangs above the court at Kaseya Center.
  • • The Heat may have eyes on more than one free agent target. An associate of Bradley Beal told a reporter "we'll see. The Heat would be an interesting spot." Meanwhile, Khris Middleton has been linked to Miami because of his connection with Giannis Antetokounmpo, but was not targeted by the Heat early in free agency, per a league source.
  • • Malik Beasley pleaded not guilty to multiple federal charges including conspiracy to commit wire fraud, money laundering and bribery, and was released on a $100,000 bond with his parents as suretors. His attorney said Beasley "looks forward to fighting" and is "presumed innocent."
  • • The NBA laid off dozens of employees Wednesday, per Sports Business Journal, as the league reallocates resources toward NBA Europe and its local TV business. The cuts will impact several business departments and support staff, but will also translate to new strategic hires.
  • • Victor Oladipo took to social media to announce he is a free agent without an agent, saying he and his family are weighing opportunities directly. "I'd rather show you I'm ready than spend my time trying to explain why," Oladipo wrote.
  • • The Kings signed second-round pick Emanuel Sharp to a three-year, $6.35 million deal with two fully guaranteed years, and also agreed to a two-way contract with Adam Flagler, who spent the previous two seasons with Oklahoma City. The Hawks signed No. 52 pick Henri Veesaar to a four-year, $9.3 million deal and inked Zuby Ejiofor to his rookie scale contract, the first signing of the 2026 draft class.
  • • Chris Paul is fueling comeback speculation after a workout video surfaced alongside Celtics star Jayson Tatum.
