NBA Rumors Wrap: Cavaliers may exit Harden talks, Edwards and Ball fund Paris trip, 2026-27 schedule revealed

The Cleveland Cavaliers are showing signs of walking away from sign-and-trade negotiations for Jonathan Kuminga and Peyton Watson as frustration builds in James Harden's camp over the pace of his contract situation, per Jake Fischer. Harden's representatives expect him to be paid "handsomely," and the longer the Cavaliers delay, the more likely talks collapse entirely. Watson's market is further complicated by a separate situation in Los Angeles, where the Clippers are waiting on the conclusion of the Kawhi Leonard investigation before moving forward on any deal that could include Watson. One potential scenario would allow the Clippers to expand the Leonard trade to acquire Watson without sending salary back to Denver. That leaves both Watson and Kuminga in a holding pattern until at least two separate front offices resolve their own outstanding business.

Here are more notes from around the league:
  • The NBA released its Opening Night schedule for October 20: Celtics at Pistons (3 p.m. ET on NBC), Sixers at Knicks, and Thunder at Spurs. The Knicks game doubles as New York's championship ring ceremony. The early Celtics-Pistons tip is a deliberate choice, with league officials viewing it as a way to capture primetime viewership in Europe as the NBA prepares to launch a separate league there next season.
  • The marquee matchup of opening week comes Wednesday, October 21: Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo host LaMelo Ball and Anthony Edwards in Miami on ESPN. Giannis is already looking forward to it. "I'm gonna put on a show," he said. "I'm going to get a win too."
  • Christmas Day 2026 on ABC and ESPN features five games: Spurs at Knicks, Heat at Celtics, 76ers at Lakers, Thunder at Timberwolves, and Nuggets at Warriors. The full schedule drops Thursday, August 13.
  • Anthony Edwards and LaMelo Ball have taken it upon themselves to build chemistry with the new-look Timberwolves roster. According to team sources, the two stars offered to personally cover the cost of flying the entire team to Paris for summer workouts. "He's extremely excited about the Ball deal," coach Chris Finch said.
  • Jalen Brunson played through a left wrist injury during the NBA Finals and has since had surgery. He opened up about the decision at his charity golf classic Monday: "I just wasn't going to let something that seemed minor take me out." His six-to-eight week recovery timeline puts him on track for training camp in September.
  • The Knicks missed an extension deadline with Miles McBride on Tuesday. If McBride signs an extension after today, he cannot be traded before the February deadline. Extensions for Karl-Anthony Towns and Josh Hart are a different situation: their deals would not necessarily remove their trade eligibility depending on the structure.
  • Warriors GM Mike Dunleavy Jr. said he is "pretty confident" Steph Curry will finish his career in Golden State. "If he comes to me and wants to be moved, Joe and I will talk it through," Dunleavy said. "We wouldn't love it. But he's earned the right to do what he wants." On the roster-building side, Dunleavy said the team prioritized talent over availability this offseason and remains confident it can improve the roster mid-season if needed.
  • Dunleavy also said the Warriors hope Draymond Green, who re-signed on a one-year deal this summer, finishes his career in Golden State as well. "He's no different than Steph in the sense of we hope he retires a Warrior," Dunleavy said. Separately, Dunleavy said he remains optimistic a deal with Brandin Podziemski will get done before the season begins, with no update yet on return timelines for Jimmy Butler or Moses Moody.
  • Nikola Jokic's agent Misko Raznatovic confirmed that Panathinaikos owner Dimitris Giannakopoulos made a genuine offer to bring Jokic to Athens. "In 30 years of working in this business, I have never encountered an offer like that," Raznatovic said. Giannakopoulos confirmed the proposal was made to both Jokic and the Nuggets and was rejected.
  • Norman Powell revealed in an interview that he expected to re-sign with the Heat before Miami landed Giannis. "We heard some rumblings that 'Norm, Bam and Giannis sounds really good,'" Powell said. "But when the trade is on the table... it seemed like they were like, 'If you want Giannis, you have to take Bobby Portis.'" Portis fired back on social media: "I ain't no throw in piece. From champ to champ respectfully king."
  • A federal judge revoked the bond of Deniro Laster, a childhood friend of former Heat guard Terry Rozier and a co-defendant in the NBA gambling case, after he posted rat emojis on Instagram and contacted co-defendants and potential witnesses in violation of a no-contact order. Separately, a DOJ lawyer clarified in court that the government is not alleging Rozier is a gang member, walking back language in a prior filing that raised that implication.
  • Michael Wilbon, who has known Michael Jordan for more than 40 years, pushed back on the idea that Jordan's limited involvement with NBA on NBC was a surprise. "Like Michael was gonna be on a set five wide? Come on, stop," Wilbon said. "Networks do hype bullsh*t. All of them. They're selling."
  • The family that controls Tottenham Hotspur is competing with the club's former chairman for the NBA Europe's $1 billion London franchise. The Lewis family has joined forces with the Checketts family (Real Salt Lake, St. Louis Blues) and the Eccles family, which has ties to the 2002 and 2034 Salt Lake City Winter Olympics.
  • Ben Simmons is expected to be in some NBA team's training camp this fall, potentially without any guaranteed money attached. "I'm basically expecting Ben Simmons to be in some NBA team's training camp come this fall," Jake Fischer said.
  • Tyrese Maxey said he cold-called LeBron James this summer to recruit him to Philadelphia. "You arguably the greatest of all-time so it's like why wouldn't we want you on our team," Maxey told him. Maxey also acknowledged he will have to adjust his game with LeBron and Jaylen Brown on the roster, and said the team does not expect LeBron to play 70 games this season.
