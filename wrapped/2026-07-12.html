NBA Rumors Wrap: LeBron James' free agency draws East interest, Bucks re-sign Trent Jr., Herro on Bucks trade **LeBron James'** free agency is pulling the NBA's attention toward the Eastern Conference, with Marc Stein reporting that James is "looking at Eastern Conference destinations with much more seriousness" and is "apparently open to going very, very far away from where he's been for the last 8 years." **Cleveland**, **Miami** and **Philadelphia** have emerged as the primary landing spots being discussed. ESPN's Brian Windhorst added fuel to the fire Friday, saying he had "somebody on the phone with me today that I consider a good source who was swearing it's a done deal in another city [outside of Cleveland]," though Windhorst cautioned he was "being very, very cautious" given the noise. The **Golden State Warriors** remain very much in the conversation, too — reporter Jake Fischer said the idea of LeBron in San Francisco "is something that is still very much on the table." Rich Paul, James' agent, offered his own framing on the decision: "He's not chasing the ring. He's got four. There's nothing to chase." Here are more notes from around the league: • **Gary Trent Jr.** agreed to a four-year, $64 million fully guaranteed deal to stay with the **Milwaukee Bucks**, per Shams Charania. The two sides had been sorting through sign-and-trade possibilities from interested teams over the past week before ultimately moving toward completing a new deal together Saturday. Klutch Sports CEO Rich Paul and agent Lucas Newton worked with Bucks officials on the agreement. • **Tyler Herro** said he saw the trade to Milwaukee coming even before it happened. "If any summer, it was going to be this summer, so I was anticipating this," Herro told South Florida reporters. "I'm excited and happy it's Milwaukee." Herro also acknowledged his years of trade speculation in Miami: "I've said it though, Miami is an organization that wants to win championships and they want to compete." • Shams Charania said there was a strike to the face area involving Herro in an altercation with **Bam Adebayo**, and that the league and players union would have involvement in the coming days. Marc Stein, for his part, said Adebayo is untouchable in Miami's front office view: "He is Heat royalty for Micky Arison, Nick Arison, Pat Riley. He is the modern day Udonis Haslem." • **Charlotte Hornets** president of basketball operations **Jeff Peterson** called the LaMelo Ball trade "probably the most difficult" decision he's had to make, framing it as a long-term play toward contention. "The goal is never to compete for a play-in spot," Peterson said. He added: "LaMelo was tremendous in our run last year. He was extremely impactful and I know he's going to do great in Minnesota. But for the Charlotte Hornets, just felt like it was the right time." Peterson also said the team wants **Brandon Miller** signed long-term: "We want Brandon Miller here for a very long time." • **Derrick White** found out **Jaylen Brown** had been traded to the **76ers** while home with his wife. "It's tough to see someone get traded, and JB's been an amazing teammate, amazing friend to me," White said. His message to Brown was simple: "Tell him thank you for everything he did for me, and just know how much he means to me." **Neemias Queta**, who's played alongside Brown for three years, said: "Jaylen's a guy that you can't replace in the city. Really, really hard to replace on the court as well." • The Celtics story has a warmer subplot. Joe Mazzulla flew to Lisbon before Queta's contract extension to work out with the center at the gym where he first learned to play basketball. "A couple years ago, if you had said an NBA coach is coming to that specific gym just to see me and get to know me, I would have been like, 'You gotta be kidding me,'" Queta said. Mazzulla also traveled to Madrid in early July to support **Hugo Gonzalez** during World Cup qualifying, attending every practice and going to dinner with the young guard. • **Victor Wembanyama** passed on the supermax clause in his extension with the **Spurs**, forgoing a projected $301 million over five years to preserve the team's flexibility under the second apron. NBPA executive director David Kelly addressed it directly: "Our position would be that the system should not require a player to carry all that burden." Kelly also called for the second apron to be "softened" or removed ahead of the 2028 CBA opt-out window. • The NBPA's criticism of the second apron isn't new, but Kelly put it bluntly at his introductory press conference Friday. "We are not fans of the second apron," Kelly said. "We did not propose the second apron. We should have done a better job of fighting back against it. In the future, we will have a much more unified union, and we will do a better job." • **Luke Kornet** recorded a two-minute voice memo pitch to LeBron James at Rich Paul's request, re-introducing himself and listing San Antonio's selling points: no state income tax, SeaWorld, and a local bakery called La Panadería. Kornet also called himself "a generational player" in the video and cited Wembanyama as another draw. • **Ja Morant** is still getting used to his new team colors after the trade from Memphis to Portland. Catching sight of his new jersey number for the first time, his reaction was straightforward: "Oh my God, I signed with the number 12!" Morant also said new teammate **Deni Avdija** is the biggest ragebaiter in the locker room: "Deni definitely number one, but he for sure likes to ragebait people to try to make you respond, or get you mad, so when we have to play or something, you have an edge already." • **Paolo Banchero** said his early connection with new Magic coach **Sean Sweeney** has been strong. Sweeney flew to Seattle to work out with Banchero for two days last week. "Me and him have really hit it off so far," Banchero said. "I'm just really excited about what he can do for me and the team." • **Victor Oladipo** announced a pro day workout next Tuesday in Las Vegas as part of his NBA comeback attempt. The event is open to front office executives, scouts, coaches, and league personnel. • Chicago Bulls rookie **Caleb Wilson** cried before his Las Vegas Summer League debut, his first game in five months. "I felt terrible because my team lost in the tournament and my coach got fired. It was a lot for me at that point," Wilson said. He went out and scored 35 points with five rebounds and three blocks in a 97-96 loss to the Grizzlies. • **Norman Powell**, now with the Bulls, said he wanted to return to Miami but understands the business realities of the salary cap. "I go in every situation wanting to be where I'm at and help win," Powell said. "I've never needed 25 shots to get my stuff off. I play within the flow of the game. I try to play the game the right way." • Hall of Famer **Bob McAdoo** received the 2026 Tex Winter Assistant Coach Lifetime Impact Award from the National Basketball Coaches Association. McAdoo thanked Pat Riley, who gave him his start as an assistant coach, and the Arison family in his acceptance remarks.
