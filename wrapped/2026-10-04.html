NBA Rumors Wrap: LeBron praises Embiid's health, Duren extension announced, Knicks unlikely to extend Towns

LeBron James offered an early endorsement of his new co-star Friday, saying Joel Embiid is a fundamentally different player than the one he played alongside at the 2024 Paris Olympics. "The difference between '24 and now, he's just so much more healthier," LeBron said after a Blue vs. White scrimmage with the Sixers. "He hasn't missed a practice. He's been in damn near everything that we've done." The comments carry weight given how Embiid's availability has defined every conversation about Philadelphia's ceiling for years. Sixers GM Mike Gansey added that LeBron has set the tone from day one, arriving first and leaving last, to the point where teammates are recalibrating their own routines around him. Philadelphia opens its regular season October 20 against the Knicks, a game averaging a league-high $2,088 per ticket on resale.

One small detail from camp stood out off the court. Per Jake Fischer, LeBron switched his practice jersey number from his usual Olympic No. 6 to No. 9 out of respect for Julius Erving's retired number in Philadelphia.

Here are more notes from around the league:
  • • Jalen Duren and the Pistons have officially announced a contract extension, with an introductory press conference scheduled for Sunday. The deal comes after a rocky restricted free agency that Shams Charania said created a real rift between player and team. The mending process started in earnest after Duren showed up to Cade Cunningham's birthday party in Los Angeles and met face-to-face with owner Tom Gores to discuss how he wanted to move forward.
  • • The Knicks appear headed toward a stalemate on multiple extension fronts. It seems unlikely that New York will reach deals with Karl-Anthony Towns, Josh Hart or Miles McBride before the regular season, with a clear gap between what the team is offering Towns and what he's seeking. Hart was candid about what that means day-to-day: "I'm sure there will probably be a little extra weight to the jersey. You don't know the certainty of your situation and obviously it affects home life."
  • • Giannis Antetokounmpo played for the Heat for the first time Saturday night in the NBA's preseason opener against the Raptors in Quebec City. Kawhi Leonard, newly reacquired by Toronto, sat out as part of his ramp-up plan and is also unlikely to play next weekend against the Clippers in Vancouver.
  • • Bam Adebayo made a case for patience on the new Heat frontcourt: "People are under appreciating it right now because they haven't seen it, but as the time goes by people will learn to appreciate what we will do," he said of his partnership with Giannis. Meanwhile, Pelle Larsson signed a four-year, $60 million extension with Miami two weeks ago, and coach Erik Spoelstra credited the Giannis effect for helping open doors: "The respect level for how they both compete. I think that's what opened up Giannis' eyes toward Pelle."
  • • Trae Young went on record about his defensive limitations with the Wizards, saying "I'm probably going to be the weak link defensively, but I can still give effort." He added that Washington's collection of rim protectors gives him more coverage than he's had in the past. On his new teammate Anthony Davis, Young was direct: "Anthony Davis is such a professional and a top 75 player for a reason of all time," he said, predicting Davis will be ready and motivated from the jump.
  • • Anthony Edwards arrived at Timberwolves camp in a good mood. "It's going to sound like I say this every year, but I haven't been more excited for a season to come," he said, pointing to the team's defensive potential. New addition Ayo Dosunmu has been trash-talking his way through practice, and Edwards approves: "Not many guys talk trash back to me, so having somebody like him on my team, not against me, is super great."
  • • Isaiah Jackson has been running with the Clippers' first unit and is confident about where he stands. "I think I'm going to be in the starting role," he said, adding that his Achilles is fully healed and he knows Ty Lue's system far better than he did a year ago. Coach Tyronn Lue hasn't officially named starters, saying Brook Lopez "will still be involved in that as well so we don't quite know right now." Guard Kobe Sanders will miss the Clippers' preseason opener against Golden State due to a left hip impingement.
  • • Luka Doncic singled out Adou Thiero when asked about underrated players on the Lakers' roster. "What I saw from Adou, like you said, this year and the improvement is insane. So, I'm very excited for him," Doncic said. Lakers wing Ziaire Williams had his own big moment off the court, proposing to his girlfriend at a Bruno Mars concert at SoFi Stadium.
  • • Steve Kerr on Stephen Curry's contract extension: "Steph is obviously still making a ton of money, but he's very unselfish and understands that this pickup will allow us next summer to sign better players, more players."
  • • Draymond Green pushed back on Karl-Anthony Towns Sr.'s suggestion that his son's championship run with the Knicks puts him ahead of Jimmy Butler. "Jimmy is Jimmy. Jimmy's going to be Jimmy," Green said on his podcast. "Jimmy still has a chance to get a championship. So what I would say to the OG is, if Jimmy gets the championship, where are they then?"
  • • The Sixers' jersey patch market is booming on the back of LeBron's arrival. Bloom Energy will pay more than $30 million annually for the patch, a top-two deal league-wide. StubHub says Sixers game searches now account for 38% of all NBA searches, up from 4% a year ago.
  • • The Knicks are prioritizing adding a center for their final roster spot, with Tony Bradley brought into camp to compete against James Wiseman and Drew Eubanks for the position.
  • • Keon Ellis told reporters that playing for Jordi Fernandez was the primary reason he signed with the Nets. "It's a safe space right there. He's familiar with my game, I'm familiar with how he rocks. That was the No. 1 thing," Ellis said. Fernandez returned the compliment, calling Ellis the player with "the best hands in the NBA."
  • • At least 15 Pacers games will air on free over-the-air television in central Indiana this season under a new deal between DAZN and Nexstar Media Group.
  • • Cameron Payne has signed with Anadolu Efes in Turkey through the end of the 2026-27 season after spending last year at Partizan.
