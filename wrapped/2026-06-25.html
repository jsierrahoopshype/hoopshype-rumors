NBA Rumors Wrap: LaMelo Ball trade talks heat up, Pistons go big-game hunting, Sabonis on the block

The Charlotte Hornets are actively engaged in trade talks surrounding LaMelo Ball as multiple teams pursue the star guard, per Shams Charania. The Minnesota Timberwolves and Toronto Raptors have emerged as the two most aggressive suitors, with Minnesota expressing a "strong desire" to acquire him and Toronto renewing conversations that first began in the summer of 2025. Ball himself has reportedly been looking forward to the coming Hornets season, but Charlotte is nonetheless listening to offers. Any deal involving the Wolves could get complicated quickly, as the Randle-to-Brooklyn trade that Minnesota already agreed to is not expected to become official for several weeks while both teams work through other moves under CBA rules.

The financial mechanics of a potential Wolves-Ball deal are already being mapped out. One framework would see Minnesota expand the Julius Randle trade into a four-team deal that includes Charlotte, using Donte DiVincenzo to complete a 100 percent salary match so the Wolves can stay below the first apron, then add draft picks and young players to sweeten the offer for the Hornets. If that structure comes together, Randle would still land in Brooklyn. Charlotte's asking price and timeline remain unclear.

Here are more notes from around the league:
  • The Detroit Pistons are set to aggressively pursue star free agents and trade targets after shipping Isaiah Stewart to Memphis for three future second-round picks. Brian Windhorst said on ESPN's draft broadcast that Detroit had interest in Austin Reaves before the Lakers locked him up, and mentioned Kawhi Leonard and LeBron James as names the Pistons could chase. The Stewart trade clears salary, and the Pistons could free up as much as $33 million in cap space if they waive Duncan Robinson's partially guaranteed deal.
  • The Sacramento Kings are exploring the trade market at multiple positions. Brian Windhorst said on the draft broadcast that there is "a lot of interest" in Domantas Sabonis and the Kings may look to see what they can get for him as they look to reshape the roster. Chris Haynes separately reported that Sacramento is also looking to move DeMar DeRozan.
  • Kawhi Leonard's trade situation is drawing some creative speculation. Bobby Marks noted during ESPN's draft coverage that his former teams could be the most likely destinations, which points to either San Antonio or Toronto. The Raptors already have trade interest in Ball, so how aggressively they pursue Leonard alongside that remains to be seen.
  • Knicks president Leon Rose broke his long public silence on the Jalen Brunson signing and the Tom Thibodeau firing, appearing on Brunson and Josh Hart's "Roommates Show" podcast after promising he would if New York won the title. On firing Thibodeau: "It was tough. Thibs is a great coach. Did so much for the organization. We were right on the doorstep. Just felt that, just needed a change in voice, a change in philosophy." On the early criticism of the Brunson signing: "I got killed about Jalen, and now everyone thinks I'm a genius."
  • Barack Obama praised Finals MVP Brunson at length, calling out his "toughness and endurance and mental fortitude." Obama also weighed in on Victor Wembanyama, saying he believes Wemby "will end up being as good as we are projected" but that he needs to develop a go-to move, add weight, and improve his conditioning after watching him get tired during the All-Star Game.
  • The Denver Nuggets feel confident they can retain restricted free agent Spencer Jones, with GM Ben Tenzer praising how the wing stepped up defensively and as a shooter. Tenzer also acknowledged the team's thin draft capital situation, saying "it's tough to not have as many [picks] as other teams," but that the organization is "comfortable with that."
  • A pair of second-round draft-night trades worth keeping track of: Atlanta traded up from No. 57 to No. 52 to grab Henri Veesaar from the Clippers, leapfrogging the Lakers, who several teams believed were also targeting the Estonian big. Separately, the Mavs officially acquired the rights to No. 56 Vsevolod Ishchenko from the Lakers for cash, with the 6-foot-8 wing averaging 8.7 points on 46.3% from three for Russia's Lokomotiv Kuban.
  • The Knicks also traded No. 53 Ugonna Onyenso to the Pistons for cash on draft night, while Orlando sent No. 46 Felix Okpara to Washington in exchange for the No. 51 and No. 60 picks. The Magic then turned around and acquired No. 60 back from Milwaukee for cash.
  • Several undrafted free agent signings from draft night: OKC landed Creighton guard Josh Dix on a two-way deal. The Heat signed Miami guard Tre Donaldson to a two-way. The Lakers agreed to a two-way with Vanderbilt forward AK Okereke and separately signed MAC Player of the Year Peter Suder, who shot 42% from three while leading Miami (OH) to a 31-0 regular season, to a two-way as well.
  • More roster-building moves from around the league: The Hornets signed Butler forward Michael Ajayi to a two-year two-way contract. The Rockets will add NC State guard Quadir Copeland on a two-way. The Bucks signed George Washington forward Rafael Castro to a two-way deal. The Nets agreed to an Exhibit 10 with Saint Louis guard Dion Brown, who shot 61.6% from the field as a senior.
  • The Pacers are signing undrafted BYU big Keba Keita to an Exhibit 10 deal ahead of Summer League. The Wizards agreed to an Exhibit 10 with Auburn forward KeShawn Murphy, who averaged two stocks per game for the Tigers. No. 1 pick AJ Dybantsa, asked what he does for fun in Washington, answered simply: "Sleep."
  • Tracy McGrady revealed in a new interview that the Bulls nearly traded for him the night before the 1997 draft as part of a deal that would have sent Scottie Pippen out of Chicago, only for Jerry Reinsdorf and Michael Jordan to kill the deal at the last moment.
  • Marcus Morris made a strong case for keeping Jaylen Brown in Boston: "I don't know if you can actually go out on the market and find a better player that plays defense and offense at the highest level." Brown has been the subject of ongoing trade speculation around the Celtics.
