NBA Rumors Wrap: Kawhi trade paused by league, LeBron closer to decision, NBA probes Gary Trent Jr. deal **LeBron James** remains the center of the basketball world with no resolution in sight, but Shams Charania says a decision could be coming soon. Per Shams, LeBron is getting closer to making a decision as next week approaches, though the picture remains murky. Charania also reported that three to five owners and GMs have sent voice memos to LeBron via agent **Rich Paul**, with the **Cleveland Cavaliers**, **Miami Heat**, and **Philadelphia 76ers** emerging as the primary contenders. Brian Windhorst added that not only does nobody in the league know where LeBron is going, but the interested teams haven't even been told what factors will drive the decision. LeBron himself said goodbye to his old team Thursday, publicly thanking Jeanie Buss and Rob Pelinka while calling his eight years with the **Lakers** "an unbelievable ride." Here are more notes from around the league: • The **Kawhi Leonard** trade between the **Clippers** and **Raptors** has hit a significant snag. Per Shams Charania, the league blocked a trade call between the two teams, pausing the deal entirely. Charania said a resolution is still weeks away. • The NBA is investigating the four-year, $64 million free-agent contract between the **Bucks** and **Gary Trent Jr.** A league spokesperson told Marc Stein the league "is continuing to look into it." The deal is fully guaranteed, with Trent earning $15.2 million in Year 1, $16.4 million in Years 2 and 3, and $16 million in Year 4. • Former No. 1 pick Joe Smith weighed in on the Trent and Kawhi situations, telling ScoopB.com that his own infamous 2000 scandal keeps getting dragged back into the headlines. "If they're going to keep bringing my name up every time a situation comes up… then they might as well just call it the 'Joe Smith Effect' or the 'Joe Smith Clause,'" Smith said. He was quick to add that his situation was different: "I didn't understand what was going on at the time at all. I just got thrown into the fire." • **Giannis Antetokounmpo** was formally introduced as a member of the **Heat** on Thursday, and he arrived with a rush of emotion. "Everything is new. I think until last night when I got off the plane, it didn't hit me yet," he said. Giannis told the room he called Pat Riley after the trade and was vulnerable with him: "I know you think I'm this big tough beast, but at the end of the day I'm human. I have my emotions and I was like, 'I'm scared. This is different.'" • Giannis said he's ready to be coached hard by **Erik Spoelstra**. "I'd rather you tell me the ugly truth than a beautiful lie," he said. He also described his first workout with **Bam Adebayo** in Las Vegas: "I knew he was a good player. After the workout I realized he's not a good player, he's a great player." • **Pat Riley** on giving up young players and picks to land Giannis: "I wish them nothing but the best. They're great young players. But we're about now." Riley also acknowledged that the three-week wait between the trade being agreed to and Thursday's press conference came with a cost: "For me personally, it's been three weeks of anxiety." • **Bobby Portis**, who joined Miami after six years in Milwaukee, revealed that the Bucks mentioned him in trade rumors relentlessly. "Six years. Every year," he said. Portis added that his conversations with Giannis about coming to Miami were always about winning, and that he's been a Heat fan since Ray Allen's corner three in the 2013 Finals. • With the Heat very much in the LeBron sweepstakes, Riley didn't exactly downplay the possibility Thursday. "You'll never win one if you don't have the talent and the coaching to do it," Riley said. "He knows what he wants. He knows what he has to do." Meanwhile, Shams said the **Warriors** are behind Miami, Cleveland, and Philadelphia in the AD sweepstakes, and that any Golden State pursuit of **Anthony Davis** would require packaging LeBron with another star. • **Donovan Mitchell** made his pitch to LeBron directly. "It's home, we've done it once, let's do it again, simple as that," Mitchell said. The Phillies' Bryce Harper, asked about LeBron's destination during a game Thursday, picked Cleveland too, though he gave the Sixers a plug: "They got Jaylen Brown. You wanna come to a crazy city and have some fun? This is it." • LeBron, for his part, reflected on his own basketball mortality Thursday. "My favorite basketball player of all-time and my inspiration was Michael Jordan. There was an end for him. If there was an end for one of the greatest to ever play the game of basketball, it's inevitable it's going to happen to all of us." He also joked that he could still average 10 points in the league until age 65, if only refs would call his fouls. • Momentum is building between the **Pistons** and **Jalen Duren** on a max extension. Jake Fischer reported that things have moved in a positive direction since Detroit cleared cap space in the Caris LeVert trade, which brought back Taurean Prince and Gary Harris and opened room for Duren's 25% max deal. • The Heat and center **Vlad Goldin** have agreed to a two-way contract, per HoopsHype. Goldin averaged 12.3 points, 7.6 rebounds, and 1.9 blocks in nine games for Miami last season, playing primarily with the team's G League affiliate in Sioux Falls. • **Trae Young**'s agent Aaron Mintz explained the thinking behind Young's extension with the **Wizards**. Young had an existing relationship with the front office, most of whom have Oklahoma City roots, and was further sold after Washington landed the No. 1 pick and drafted AJ Dybantsa. "There is an excitement in being part of a sleeping giant in that market," Mintz said. • **Draymond Green** announced he'll switch to No. 5 if LeBron James ends up in Golden State. • Marc Spears said on ESPN that **Jaylen Brown** felt like the second option in Boston throughout his time there. "In a lot of ways Jaylen probably felt like Jayson [Tatum] was the favorite," Spears said, in the wake of Brown's trade to Philadelphia. • **Victor Wembanyama** has already resumed physical training back in France, working with his personal trainer under what's being described as an "experimentation" approach. He's expected to join the French national team in August for the second qualifying window for the 2027 World Cup. • **Jalen Brunson** said Thursday that defending a championship is an even bigger challenge than winning one. "Now it's a target on your back," he said. The champion **Knicks** have already lost center Mitch Robinson this offseason. • **Zaire Wade** pleaded not guilty to a felony domestic violence charge in a Pasadena courtroom Thursday. Prosecutors told the judge that an unregistered firearm was found at the scene following his arrest last month. • On this date in summer league history: Anthony Morrow scored 47 points for the Warriors in 2009, a record that still stands at the Las Vegas tournament. Steph Curry, a teammate on that summer squad, remembered shooting 32.5 percent and being lousy. Morrow made 18 of 26 from the field, including 7 of 9 from three. "I got in the groove that day, and it really kind of set the tone for the rest of my career," Morrow said.
